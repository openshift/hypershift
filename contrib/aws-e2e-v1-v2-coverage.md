## Evidence and counting rules

The fixed v1 baseline is
[`dbb770cf30601c65f414ad1c39e184809e31068b`](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/tree/dbb770cf30601c65f414ad1c39e184809e31068b/test/e2e).
The initial v2 comparison revision is
[`a0aaf80a611bed96903d93ec9e7859f231f28488`](https://github.com/openshift/hypershift/tree/a0aaf80a611bed96903d93ec9e7859f231f28488/test/e2e/v2).
V1 links below are immutable. Relative v2 links identify the implementation in
the revision containing this document; file-level links avoid stale line anchors while
integration is changing. They must be reviewed again after changes.

**This ledger is in progress, not a parity or runtime-success declaration.**
The entrypoint census is complete; behavioral accounting is being corrected
for retained recovery/chaos callers and source-applicability defects. Both feature/table
inventories are integrated below, with additional shared-helper audits. Complete
transitive equivalence and final integrated-code validation remain incomplete.
All AWS execution, including cleanup and
optional configurations, is **unrun** in this investigation. Source inspection,
unit tests, compilation, and Ginkgo selection are not AWS execution evidence.

Counts use three different units, which must not be added together:

- **25 top-level test entrypoints**, excluding `TestMain` and ordinary helper
  unit tests. Six have an unconditional-disable or non-AWS originating
  execution. Nineteen are AWS-selectable, including two roots whose intended
  control-plane assertions use a `NonePlatform` guest without an AWS-excluding
  root guard. Guest platform alone is not the root's invocation applicability.
- **Entrypoint rows E01–E25** summarize the census, not atomic parity credit.
- **Cross-cutting rows H01–H38 and D01–D18** identify 56 hook/lifecycle
  obligations. These may also be reached by multiple entrypoints; do not count
  repeated callers as extra coverage. Feature-domain assertion rows must be
  reconciled separately before an exhaustive behavioral total can be stated.
- **Suite rows S01–S10** additionally identify ten configuration, shared-resource,
  diagnostic and suite-entry obligations. Diagnostics are not successful feature
  assertions. The cross-cutting plus suite inventory currently has 66 rows.

Disposition vocabulary: **source-equivalent** = the bounded row outcome has
matching or stronger native assertions, meaningful inputs, cleanup and an
executable selection/configuration path verified in source. This is independent
of runtime, which remains UNRUN. Feature-row credit does not silently credit
canonical H/D/S root obligations. **candidate** = a concrete selected v2 implementation
exists but full equivalence remains under review; **partial** = an identified
assertion/configuration is missing or its originating-caller equivalence audit
is incomplete, as distinguished in the row; **missing** = no counterpart located;
**deferred/unresolved** = retained lifecycle/isolation behavior is not implemented
and is deferred from this remediation pass; no equivalence or exclusion credit
is assigned, and no redesign is included. **excluded**
= immutable source proves non-AWS or unconditional disablement. A candidate is
not credited as equivalent. Missing credentials, images, flags, prerequisites,
pending specs, skips, or informing failures never justify an exclusion or a pass.

Current suite/cross-cutting disposition counts are stated with the final
consolidated row accounting below. Source-equivalent row credit is bounded by
the explicit assertion/configuration evidence; it is not an executed/pass count
or a claim of complete originating-root parity.
E19/E20 remain AWS-selectable baseline obligations. Existing legacy v2 etcd
recovery bodies are preserved; separate native assertions now have dedicated
HA/zero-worker fixtures and explicit configuration. Candidate counts are not
pass counts. The former171-equivalent behavioral tally wrongly excluded six
retained chaos/recovery records; the current accounting below reconciles them and
individually reconciles the corrected source/caller predicates. A row total is
derived from those obligations, not a fixed target.
